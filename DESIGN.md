# Crea y Monetiza landing design

The supplied advent calendar is the visual source of truth: burgundy #4A0D22,
pink #F18BC4, cream #FFF6EB, warm serif display headings and readable sans-serif
body copy. Preserve the supplied gifts, illustrations, calendar layout and local
midnight behavior. Gift one is explicitly available for public early access.

The private student entrance belongs to this landing, not the PerfectFlow CRM
shell. Use the same palette and welcoming gift vocabulary. Email first, then a
single-use six-digit code; keep the email visible, offer a clear resend/change
email action, disable duplicate submissions and show recoverable inline errors.
Labels, keyboard focus, mobile spacing and reduced-motion preferences are required.

Client rules, email delivery and CRM intake are configured in Activepieces.
Private resources must never be published in GitHub Pages. Keep the content in
an isolated frame with scoped resource URLs; the hosting gateway only verifies
signed access and serves private static files.
